# Troubleshooting cameras
- **"Camera frozen"**: the stream stopped updating — power-cycle the camera or check the NVR.
- **"Covered or dark"**: lens blocked or lights off. - **"Moved"**: confirm in the dashboard if you re-angled it on purpose, then re-check calibration.
- Existing NVR? Use ONVIF/RTSP from the NVR; no new cameras are needed in most cases.
