# Venue Signage Templates

Owners must check local rules on wording, language, size, placement and any additional requirements (for example, New York City signage for biometric identifier collection applies only if biometrics are collected, which TableSight is designed not to do; verify). Place signs at every entrance and in each monitored area before cameras go live, at eye level. Provide the text in each local language (add Arabic for RTL markets).

## A. Short sign (first layer)

```
CAMERAS IN USE
This venue uses cameras to help us manage seating and service.
We do not use facial recognition. Video stays [on site / for X days].
More information: [QR code / URL]  |  Questions: [privacy contact]
Operator: [VENUE LEGAL NAME]
```

## B. Pictogram guidance
Camera icon plus text. Must be readable before entering the monitored area. EU: this is the first layer of the layered approach suggested in EDPB Guidelines 3/2019 on video devices (verify).

## C. Second layer (QR page or counter leaflet)

- Who we are and how to contact us / our DPO.
- Purpose: managing seating, wait times and service quality.
- Legal basis (EU/UK): legitimate interests (verify wording with counsel).
- What is processed: video images analysed on-site; anonymous zone and timing data. No facial recognition, no audio, no age/gender inference.
- Who can see it: authorised managers. (Blurred views for other roles **[NOT YET IMPLEMENTED — must be true before publishing]** — delete this sentence until it is true.)
- Retention: [X days].
- Recipients: our software provider TableSight (processor) and [others].
- Your rights and how to complain.
- Staff: staff analytics are [off / anonymous / on]; see staff notice.
- Children: counts only **[NOT YET IMPLEMENTED — must be true before publishing]** (no minor-detection exists).

## D. Variants

- Outdoor patio: add "Camera area includes outdoor seating."
- Bar/nightclub: add "Cameras may operate in low light."
- Where staff-only areas are covered: notify staff separately; do not monitor toilets, changing or rest rooms.
- Audio: "No audio is recorded." Do not enable audio recording.
- Illinois / Texas / Washington venues: if the re-identification option is ever switched on, stop and obtain counsel guidance before using any wording, because consent rules apply.
