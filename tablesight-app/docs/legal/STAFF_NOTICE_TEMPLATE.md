# Staff Notice Template (Floor Analytics)

Employer: [VENUE LEGAL NAME]. Location: [LOCATION]. Date: [DATE]. Staff-analytics mode for this location: **[OFF / ANONYMIZED / ON]**.

## Before you issue this notice (checklist for the owner)

- [ ] Local law checked (verify with counsel): Germany works council co-determination and works agreement; France CSE consultation; Italy union agreement or labour authorisation; other EU works-council or union rules; US states requiring written notice or acknowledgement (for example New York, Connecticut, Delaware; verify), union agreements and collective bargaining.
- [ ] Data protection assessment completed where required (DPIA in the EU/UK).
- [ ] Staff-only areas and rest areas excluded from camera view.
- [ ] No audio recording.
- [ ] Notice delivered in each staff language, before the mode is switched on, with a way to ask questions.

## Notice text

**What is installed.** The venue uses cameras and TableSight software to see which tables and areas need attention and how long guests wait.

**What it does not do.** It does not use facial recognition, fingerprints or voice; it does not record audio; it does not guess your age, gender or emotions.

**What it does with staff (choose one)**
- OFF: Staff are not analysed. Staff may appear in video like anyone else; staff are not reported on.
- ANONYMIZED: Results are shown for areas or roles (for example "section 3 average greet time"), not for named people. [Individuals cannot be identified in reports.]
- ON: Results can be shown per team member for [purposes: scheduling support, coaching]. They will [not] be used for [discipline / pay decisions]. Reports are visible to [roles]. Retention: [X days].

**Why (employer's purpose and legal basis).** [Service quality and guest wait times; EU/UK: legitimate interests, verify]. 

**Who sees video and data.** [Managers], with blurred views for others; access is logged.

**Retention.** Video [X days]; metrics [Y days].

**Your rights.** You may ask what data we hold about you, ask for correction or deletion where the law allows, and raise concerns with [HR contact], your works council/union representative, or the data protection authority. No retaliation for raising concerns.

**Decisions.** No decision about your employment will be made by software alone. Alerts have confidence levels and may be wrong.

I received this notice on ____. Name: ____ Signature: ____ (acknowledging receipt, not consenting; consent is generally not a valid basis in employment in the EU, verify).
