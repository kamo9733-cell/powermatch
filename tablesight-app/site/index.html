Privacy & security

  • No facial recognition or biometric identification — by design.
  • Video is processed on-site; only table events leave the building. Cloud video is opt-in.
  • Staff analytics per location: on, anonymized, or off. Notice templates included.
  • Camera passwords encrypted, access audited, data export and deletion on request.

Legal templates are drafts; have counsel review them for your region.

FAQ

Do I need new cameras?

Usually not. We connect to existing RTSP/ONVIF cameras and many NVRs.

What if it's wrong?

It says so. Low-confidence or stale views are marked, and you can tap "wrong alert" to correct it.

Is this staff surveillance?

It's built for coaching and team balance. Individual staff reporting can be turned off per location.
